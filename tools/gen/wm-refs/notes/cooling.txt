Information

     Grease O-rings, hoses and lines in the cooling system with Kluberplus Gel (Part No.
     000.043.205.93) .

      1. Install radiator.

                 1. 1.1. Secure the radiator to the body with a yellow fixing clip.

                 2. 1.2. Screw in upper fastening screw.

                 3. 1.3. Screw in lower fastening screws.

                 4. 1.4. Tighten upper and lower fastening screws to Tightening torque 23 Nm (17
                    ftlb.) .

      2. Fit supply and return hose -1 and 3- .

                 1. 2.1. Coat O-rings lightly with Kluberplus Gel and fit hoses.
