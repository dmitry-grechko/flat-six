     The wheel bearing has a magnetised multipole seal, which acts as a pulse sender for the wheel
     speed sensor .

     seal and do not come into contact with magnetic objects (e.g. magnetised screwdriver, magnet) .

ASSEMBLING REAR SPRING STRUT


      WARNING: Preloaded or pressurized components

       Risk of squashing or bruising



→ Do not reach into the danger area .

→ Relieve the tension on components before starting work .

→ Secure components to prevent them from loosening suddenly .

     When installing new components, always check that they are assigned correctly .


===== p5547 =====
Coil springs are only available in pairs .

 1. Prepare parts for assembly.

 2. Clamp the spring strut in a suitable clamping block (tensioning device for spring struts).
    Alternatively, the spring clamp can also be held in a retaining device.

 3. Slide the bellows/additional spring assembly onto the piston rod.

 4. Push the pre-tensioned coil spring onto the vibration damper so that the end of the coil spring
    rests against the stop of the spring plate. Fit the stop plate -7- at the correct position.

    If you are using new coil springs, observe centering ring allocation.

   1. Coil spring marking -1- comprising two different colored lines (e.g. one red and one brown
      line) indicates the spring rate and spring length (depending on vehicle variant and
      equipment) .

   2. A marking -2- comprising one colored line indicates the coil spring tolerance group (load
      group) and determines the thickness of the centering ring -arrows- (tolerance

   correct centering rings for installation (for tolerance compensation) .
