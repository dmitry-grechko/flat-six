    1. Wipe off any traces of oil on the sealing faces with a clean, lint-free cloth.

      Information

          1. The new oil pump is fitted on the crankcase without a seal .

              1. 2.1. Screw in five external Torx screws M6 x 30 -1- and one M6 x 95 -2- by hand.

                 internal Torx screw (M8 x 22).

              3. 2.3. Check that the two bores are positioned horizontally in order to insert the special
                 tool.
