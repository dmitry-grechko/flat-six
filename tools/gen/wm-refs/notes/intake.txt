    2. Check intake-air distributor for leaks.

              1. 2.1. Connect compressed air hose with pressure gauge to the valve.

              2. 2.2. Build up overpressure of max. 0.8 bar in the intake system!

              3. 2.3. Coat accessible sealing points with commercially available leak-locating spray
